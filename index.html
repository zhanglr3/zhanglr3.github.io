My research focuses on Privacy-enhancing techniques, Fully homomorphic encryption (FHE), and Applied cryptography.

About

I am a Research Fellow in DTC at Nanyang Technological University, where I have worked with Prof. Huaxiong Wang and Prof. Kwok-Yan Lam since June 2021. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from The University of Hong Kong in March 2021, supervised by Prof. Siu-Ming Yiu, with a research focus on cryptography.
